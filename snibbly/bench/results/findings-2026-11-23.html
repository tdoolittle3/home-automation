• Inkling 2 Mini is the most accurate model tested, but too slow to read a prompt on this box. Its score is 41/42 on text and 6/6 on vision, tied with big-30b-thinking for the best text score. But it processes prompts at about 20 tok/s, about 13× slower than the Qwen 30B-class models. That's the cost of --no-op-offload, which it needs to fit in 8 GB. A 3.6k-token prompt takes about 3 minutes before the first token, so as an agent model with long tool prompts it isn't practical here.
  • Inkling at low effort gives the same score for a third of the work. Low effort also scores 47/48, averaging 273 tokens per answer against 821 at high effort. It finished the suite in 27 minutes against 68. Its one miss is a hard math item answered wrong, not cut off.
  • vl-30b is the best all-rounder. It scores 40/42 on text and 6/6 on vision with short answers (404 tokens each) at 21 tok/s, and it ran the fastest full suite (16.5 min). It beat coder-30b on text (40 vs 38) at the same speed, and it adds vision. That makes it worth testing in Hermes as the agent model (layer 3).
  • big-30b-thinking matches Inkling on text but at a far higher cost. It scores 41/42, averaging 2,700 tokens per answer and taking 96 minutes. That's fine for unattended cron scans, its current job.
  • Qwen3.6-35B isn't worth it here. It decodes fastest of the large models (30 tok/s) but over-thinks: three answers still hit the 16k cap (including an easy math item), and it scored 38/42 on text in 79 minutes. The heretic variant was worse: 37/42 with five answers cut off, in 106 minutes.
  • Most answers cut off at 4k were unfinished thinking. Raising the cap to 16k moved qwen36-35b from 37 to 44 and fast-8b from 30 to 35; for Inkling it changed one item. Instruct models that hit 4k were stuck in a loop, for example vl-30b listing every log line while counting.
  • The llama.cpp upgrade caused no regression. coder-30b: 305 vs 298 tok/s prefill and 20.9 vs 20.5 tok/s decode, measured with the vision captioner paused. Its score was 38/42 against 39/42 on the old build, a one-item difference within noise. vlm-8b is unchanged. Caveat: the other models' prefill was measured with the captioner sharing the CPU, which cost coder-30b about 20%, so read those prefill bars as lower bounds. Decode speed wasn't affected.
